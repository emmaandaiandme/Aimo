Frequently Asked

How much does Premium cost?
Premium pricing is set on a case-by-case basis. Join our Discord support server and open a ticket — an admin will discuss the current pricing and options with you directly.
Is Premium linked to my account or server?
Premium is linked to your Discord User ID. This means you can use all Premium commands in any server that AIMO is in — it follows you wherever you go.
Can I try Premium before buying?
Reach out in the support server and ask about trial options. The team is happy to discuss your needs and may be able to offer a short trial period.
How quickly is Premium activated after I pay?
Activation is manual and typically happens within a few hours of payment confirmation. During busy periods it may take up to 24 hours. You'll be notified in the support server.
